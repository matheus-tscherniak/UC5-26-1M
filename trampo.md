20. Simple Present ou Present Continuous?
Escolha a forma correta do verbo.

Exemplo:
I (tem que ter alguma coisa aqui) English every day.
(study / am studying)

✔ I study English every day.

a) She works at a school every day.
(works / is working)

b) She is working right now.
(works / is working)

c) They play soccer every Saturday.
(play / are playing)

d) They are playing soccer now.
(play / are playing)

e) He uses a computer every day.
(uses / is using)

f) He is using the computer at the moment.
(uses / is using)

g) We are studying programming this semester.
(study / are studying)

h) My brother usually watches TV at night.
(watches / is watching)

i) Look! My brother is watching TV now.
(watches / is watching)

j) I usually drink coffee in the morning.
(drink / am drinking)
